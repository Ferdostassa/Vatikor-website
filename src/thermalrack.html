02 · Why ThermRack

Why Data Centers Choose ThermRack

Seven reasons facility and IT teams choose ThermRack for thermal intelligence.

01 / 07

Save Cooling Energy.

Most data centers over-cool by 20–40% because they can't see where the cold air actually goes. ThermRack pinpoints overcooling zones in real time, so you can reduce CRAC output, raise set-points confidently, and cut cooling energy spend without risking equipment.

02 / 07

Increase Data Center Efficiency.

Understand exactly how heat is distributed across every row and rack. Rebalance loads, improve airflow containment, and identify underutilized capacity, turning thermal visibility into measurable PUE improvements.

03 / 07

Simple, Non-Invasive Installation.

Wireless cameras mount on or between racks in minutes, no cabling runs, no server shutdowns, no specialist trades. The edge computer connects to your existing LAN. Your IT or facilities team handles the entire deployment without disrupting a single workload.

04 / 07

No Cloud. Fully Secure On-Premise.

All thermal data is processed and stored on the local edge computer. Integration runs directly to your BMS via BACnet TCP/IP over your internal network. No internet dependency, no external data exposure, no cloud subscription, your data never leaves the building.

05 / 07

Modular and Scalable.

Start with one zone, one row, or one room, and expand without changing your core infrastructure. Add cameras as your data center grows. ThermRack scales from a single cage to a multi-row floor with no rework to the gateway or edge computer.

06 / 07

3D Thermal Map Visualization.

See temperature distribution across your racks as a live three-dimensional heatmap. Every camera feeds a continuous spatial view, cold aisles, hot aisles, bypass zones, and developing hotspots rendered visually so your team understands the full thermal picture at a glance.

07 / 07

Actionable Insights, Not Raw Data.

ThermRack doesn't just collect temperatures, it interprets them. Hot air bypass events, overcooling waste, thermal trend alerts, and capacity headroom are surfaced as clear dashboard metrics. Your team gets answers, not spreadsheets, so action follows insight immediately.

03 · System Architecture

A Complete Thermal Intelligence Network

ThermRack is a purpose-built system: wireless thermal cameras feed a gateway, which connects to an on-premise edge computer that stores all data locally and integrates with your BMS via BACnet TCP/IP. No cloud. No external dependencies. Full control stays with you.

  • Camera Wireless thermal cameras mount magnetically on rack doors in minutes. Power over Ethernet, one cable for power and data. Continuously streams thermal frames to the gateway.
  • Gateway Receives wireless data from all cameras in the network and forwards it via Ethernet to the edge computer. Supports multiple cameras per gateway for scalable deployments.
  • Edge Computer Processes all thermal data locally. Runs the ThermRack analytics engine, detecting hot air bypass, overcooling, undercooling, and airflow anomalies in real time. Stores full history on-premise.
  • BMS Integration Delivers actionable thermal insights to your Building Management System via BACnet TCP/IP. Seamlessly integrates with existing DCIM and automation platforms.
