Why Choose EHT

Six reasons facilities trust EHT for condition monitoring.

01 / 06

Reduces Costs. Improves Asset Visibility.

Catching developing faults weeks early eliminates emergency repair bills, unplanned downtime, and secondary damage. Know the health status of every monitored asset at a glance — all the time.

02 / 06

No Subscription. No Cloud Required.

All analysis runs entirely on the sensor itself. Insights are generated and delivered to your BMS without internet connectivity, cloud services, or recurring software fees. Your data stays on your site.

03 / 06

No Specialist Needed to Interpret Results.

EHT sends clear, actionable maintenance priorities — fault type, confidence level, and recommended action. No vibration analysis expertise required. Any maintenance technician can read and act on the output immediately.

04 / 06

Bolt-On. No Production Shutdown.

Mounts directly on any bearing housing in minutes. No electrical modifications, no specialist trades, no process interruption. Installation is a maintenance task, not a project.

05 / 06

One Sensor. Five Signal Channels.

Triaxial vibration, wide-band acoustic, and surface temperature captured simultaneously in one compact device. No multiple sensors to install, calibrate, or maintain.

06 / 06

Preventive Alerts Before Alarms Ever Fire.

EHT identifies and reports developing faults while conditions are still in the advisory zone — long before they escalate to a threshold alarm. You get time to plan a repair, not react to a failure.
