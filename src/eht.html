Three Signals

3 Sensors.
One Device.

Triaxial Vibration

Three-axis accelerometer captures vibration simultaneously across X, Y, and Z, mounted directly on the bearing housing where faults originate. No production shutdown, no electrical access required.

  • Imbalance & misalignment
  • Bearing wear & looseness
  • Resonance signatures

Wide-Band Acoustic

High-frequency acoustic sensor hears developing faults at ultrasonic frequencies, often weeks before vibration thresholds are ever reached. Cavitation, bearing defects, and gear mesh anomalies all have distinct sound signatures.

  • Pump cavitation
  • Bearing surface defects
  • Early gear mesh anomalies

Surface Temperature

Precision thermal probe tracks bearing housing temperature continuously. A rising temperature trend correlated with changing vibration or acoustic signals is one of the most reliable early-fault indicators.

  • Thermal overload & friction faults
  • Lubrication breakdown
  • Correlated multi-signal anomalies

On-Device ML Analysis

All three signals are fused and analysed together on the sensor, no cloud, no specialist required. EHT learns each asset's normal signature and delivers a health score, fault type, and severity directly to your BMS.

  • Health score 0–100%
  • Fault type & severity output
  • Direct BMS / SCADA integration
